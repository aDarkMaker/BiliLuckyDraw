Learn more

Real-time danmaku draw

Connect a room and the app listens automatically — a viewer sending the keyword joins the draw, deduplicated by UID, no manual list-keeping.

Watch and draw together

Stream player, room list and danmaku feed share one screen. A stalled stream is recovered automatically; the feed sticks to the bottom, pauses when you scroll up and resumes after a few idle seconds.

Two login methods

Log in by pasting a Cookie, or scan a QR code with the Bilibili mobile app. Credentials stay on your machine.

Multiple profiles

Keep a profile per scenario — keyword, winner count, rooms and background switch together.

Themes and backgrounds

Light and dark switch instantly; the spring-festival and beach backdrops are optional, and the accent color is sampled from the active image.

Tuned for busy rooms

A throttled image decode queue, Bilibili CDN thumbnails and visibility-gated mounting keep the UI responsive during danmaku bursts.
